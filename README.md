## Featured Coursework

### Generative AI & ChatGPT
**Ethical and Societal Implications of Generative AI**

This research examines ChatGPT as a real-world example of generative AI. Topics include transformer-based language models, benefits of generative AI, bias, privacy, transparency, misinformation, AI hallucinations, academic integrity, and responsible AI use.

### AI in Manufacturing
**AI-Powered Predictive Maintenance**

This industry case study explores how AI can be used in manufacturing through machine learning, IoT sensors, predictive analytics, cloud computing, and digital twins. The research focuses on using AI to identify potential equipment failures before they occur and improve manufacturing efficiency.

### AI in Healthcare
**Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare**

This research examines AI applications in healthcare, including personalized medicine, AI-assisted systems, robotic surgery, and brain-computer interfaces. It also considers ethical concerns such as privacy, bias, transparency, and human oversight.

### Artificial General Intelligence
**AGI in Healthcare**

This project explores the potential applications of Artificial General Intelligence in healthcare, including analyzing medical records, medical imaging, laboratory results, genetics, medications, and research to support clinical decision-making.

### AI & Blockchain
**Artificial Intelligence in Blockchain**

This research examines how AI and blockchain can work together to improve security, data integrity, automation, fraud detection, and decision-making. It also explores privacy, transparency, job displacement, and future applications.

### AI in Smart Cities
**Artificial Intelligence in Smart Cities**

This project examines AI applications in transportation, energy management, public safety, emergency response, waste management, and other city services. It also considers privacy, cybersecurity, fairness, and transparency.

### AI in Retail
**Artificial Intelligence in the Retail Industry**

This research explores recommendation systems, conversational AI, chatbots, computer vision, inventory management, and cashierless shopping. Ethical issues such as privacy, job displacement, fairness, and transparency are also discussed.

### AI Advancements, Future Trends & Regulation
This project examines recent AI advancements, future trends such as autonomous AI agents and AI-generated media, and differences in approaches to AI regulation.
